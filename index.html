Frequently Asked Questions

Are real psychologists actually involved?

Yes. For the Deep Focus and Bespoke Architect plans, your initial consultation is reviewed by a partnered psychologist. They create the clinical roadmap (therapy, exercises), which our engineers then code into your AI's system prompt.

How do the prompt updates work?

Mental health isn't static. In the Deep Focus plan, we review your interactions and update your AI's core instructions every 5 days. In the Bespoke plan, we review your daily report and re-engineer the prompt every 24 hours to match your exact mood and goals for the next day.

Is my data and chat history safe?

100% Yes. We use enterprise-grade cloud architecture. The clinical reports and chats are never used to train public AI models. Your conversations remain strictly private and are only accessed to update your prompt.
